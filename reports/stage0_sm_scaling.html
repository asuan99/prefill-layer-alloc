Per-token latency vs decode SM

Evidence 1 — the no-split anchor

D108 (108 SM, zero prefill contention) is as slow as D16 to within 0.5% in all 9 cells, and slower than D92. More decode SM ≠ faster: the curve is non-monotone in SM, so it cannot be measuring decode-SM binding.

Evidence 2 — the pure-Mamba control

M (SSM-only, O(1) recurrent decode — no KV scan) cannot be decode-SM-bound, yet reproduces the full 2.46× "sensitivity" curve, same shape as H. The curve is generated by something other than decode SM.

Mechanism — 5× wall collapse

The one fast point, D92, is where prefill is starved to 16 SM. Wall-time inflates 5.0–5.4× uniformly: the run serializes, it doesn't accelerate. Lower ITL is de-batching, not speed.

Clean signal — the surviving null

The only de-confounded contrast, D16 vs D108 (both full batch), = 1.00 ± 0.01 across 3 arms × 3 ctx. Decode is SM-insensitive from 16→108 SM. Confirms r0c; extends it to hybrid, in-situ serving, to 16k.
