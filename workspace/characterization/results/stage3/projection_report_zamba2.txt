ASSUMPTIONS
----------------------------------------
1. A1: Prefill SM speedup is linear in free_sm_fraction (upper bound).
    speedup_raw = 1 / (1 - free_sm_fraction). Real behaviour saturates
    after SM count exceeds compute saturation; this projection is an upper bound.

2. A2: Free SMs are fully reallocated to decode with zero fragmentation.
    In reality, GPC-granular allocation rounds SM counts upward;
    partial SMs are wasted.

3. A3: Measured overlap_ratio (from coexec_microbench, single isolated kernel pair)
    is representative of the full serving workload mixture.
    Real workloads have variable seq_len and batch; overlap may differ.

4. A4: ctx_switch_overhead is the only latency penalty per layer boundary.
    Other serialization costs (e.g. stream synchronization, TLB flush) ignored.

5. A5: SSM layer fraction is fixed (from model config). In practice,
    layer mix during chunked prefill may vary with batch composition.
